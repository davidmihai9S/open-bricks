### 2. Data we collect

When you use the Bot, we store:
* **Discord identifiers:** your user ID and username, and the IDs of servers, channels, and roles the Bot is configured in.
* **Game data:** your card collection, OBCoins balance, bank, loans and debt, items, purchase and transaction history, daily and streak progress, cooldowns, game XP and levels, tasks, clan membership, and leaderboard standing.
* **Social data you choose to create:** birthday, relationship status, roleplay and proposal preferences, AFK status, wishlists, favorite card, and profile settings.
* **Custom cards:** the name and image you submit, and who created and owns the card.
* **Moderation data:** warnings, moderation actions, and related records in servers that use the Bot's moderation features.
* **Server activity counts:** message counts, member joins and leaves, and invite usage, in servers where those features are enabled.
* **Recovery data:** if you run `/2fa`, we store a hashed recovery token, never the token itself, and the time it was created.
* **Account linking:** if you link an alt account, the link between the two Discord IDs.

**What we read but do not keep:**  
The Bot reads message content to detect commands, count messages for spawns, run text games, and read answers. We do not store the text of your messages, except when submitted as part of a feature, such as a custom card name, a suggestion, or an AFK reason.

**Logs:**  
We keep technical logs and error reports (command names and error details) to run and fix the Bot. Some servers' admin and log channels may receive records of commands or actions, such as roleplay use, game wins, and account restores, with user IDs and names.

### 3. Why we use it and our legal grounds
We use your data to run the game and features, keep leaderboards and statistics, prevent cheating and abuse, provide moderation tools, restore accounts, fix bugs, and improve the Bot. 

Our legal grounds are:
* Providing the Bot service you chose to use (contract);
* Our legitimate interests in preventing abuse, keeping the Bot secure, and fixing problems;
* Your consent where you give it, for example by submitting a birthday or a custom card.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

---

### 4. Where it is stored and who can see it
Data is stored in cloud databases (Turso) and on our hosting provider's servers. Pictures for cards and custom cards are stored in a separate cloud database. These providers process data for us. Some of our providers (Turso, our hosting provider, and Discord) may be located outside the EEA. Where that happens, your data is protected through the safeguards those providers use for transfers, such as standard contractual clauses. 

Leaderboards and some commands show your Discord username and game stats to other users. Server administrators and bot staff can see data needed to run and moderate the Bot. We only share data when required by law, to protect the Bot and its users, or with Discord as part of normal operation. Using the Bot also means your data passes through Discord's own services, under Discord's Privacy Policy.

---

### 5. How long we keep it
We keep game data for as long as the Bot runs and you may use it, so your progress is not lost. Logs are kept only as long as needed to run and debug the Bot. You can request deletion of your data as described below.

### 6. Your rights
You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive a copy of it in a portable form, and withdraw consent. Requesting full data removal permanently deletes your cards, currency, and progress. You can also use the Bot's built-in controls, such as `/rpblock`, `/foreversingle`, deleting your custom cards, or removing the Bot from your server. 

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at `md8884032@gmail.com`. We may need to confirm that you control the Discord account, and we answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the Romanian data protection authority, **ANSPDCP** (*Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal*), at [dataprotection.ro](https://www.dataprotection.ro), or to the authority in your own country.

---

### 7. Children
The Bot is not meant for anyone under 16. Under Romanian law, a child under 16 cannot give consent to online data processing without a parent's permission. If you believe a younger child has used the Bot, contact us and we will remove the data.

### 8. Security
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, such as secure access tokens and hashing for recovery tokens. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
